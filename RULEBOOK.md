# Faceless Clip Channel Rulebook

**Researched:** 29 Sep 2026. Based on live YouTube data from ~30 faceless clip channels, metadata for 90 of their top Shorts, and 15 top videos watched frame by frame with timestamped transcripts.

Use this as the rulebook for every video we publish. Every rule here comes from something observed in channels that are winning right now. Where a rule is a judgment call rather than a measured number, it says so.

---

## 0. How this was researched (and its limits)

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Channel discovery | YouTube channel search for 30+ queries: streamer names, "podcast clips", "kick clips", "streamer moments", Hindi podcast clips, and others |
| Performance data | Each channel's **Popular** and **Latest** Shorts and long-form tabs: titles, views, length, upload date, tags, descriptions |
| "Watching" | Played each video in a mobile player, captured frames at several timestamps (0–3s hook, middle, end) and pulled the auto-caption transcript with timestamps |
| Contrast | Compared each channel's best videos with its **latest** uploads, which use the same editor and style. The gap between the two shows what the **content** contributes on top of the **edit** |

**Limits:** I read audio through transcripts and couldn't hear music, SFX or voice energy directly. Music was identified from YouTube's audio tags where they were shown. View counts are a snapshot from 29 Sep 2026.

## 2. The big picture (read this first)

1. **Shorts are the engine.** Every channel above was built on Shorts. Long-form on streamer-clip channels mostly flops (Streamer Clip Vault's documentaries got 1K–100K). Long-form works only for **podcast** clips on dark or taboo curiosity topics (see §10).
2. **Views follow a power law.** The same channel's latest uploads usually get 5K–500K. Its top uploads get 10M–300M. You're not making one video, you're buying **lottery tickets with good odds**. **Volume × hit rate** is the game: winners run 500–1,700+ uploads.
3. **The moment matters more than the edit.** Inside one channel, the editing is identical between a 5K video and a 100M video. The difference is **what happened in the clip and who is in it**. Picking the moment is most of the job.
4. **One personality per channel.** Every winner is built around one creator or one duo (Speed, Speed+Kai, Jynxzi, MrBeast, Theo Von). Channels that clip "all streamers" (LiveStreamFails, "Streamer Clips") are weak.
5. **The edit sets the ceiling.** Raw moment channels top out around 30–110M. The cinematic story edit (deagzzz) reaches **200–313M**. More transformation gives more reach **and** protects monetization (§11).

## 3. The six winning formats (archetypes)

Pick one **primary** format per channel. Mixing formats confuses the audience and the algorithm.

### A. Raw moment clip (easiest, most common)
*SpeedKaiClip, Theo Rogan Shorts, Full Send, Whatever, Fresh&Fit*
- One continuous moment, **15–50s**, starts mid-action, ends on the punchline or reaction.
- 9:16 crop on the speaker's face. When two people talk, use a **stacked split-screen**: person A on top, person B on the bottom.
- Word-by-word captions do the heavy lifting.
- Observed: SpeedKaiClip's 109M clip opens at 0.0s with Speed saying he's about to join Kai's stream. The premise lands in the first sentence.

### B. Cinematic story edit (highest ceiling)
*deagzzzshorts (313M), MrBeastClips (66M)*
- **55–60s**, assembled from **multiple sources**: stream footage, broadcast angles, fan videos, the other person's POV.
- A **3-act arc**: setup (the problem or ask), turn, emotional payoff. Examples:
  - A fan loses something → a stranger goes to find it → it's returned → hug.
  - An ordinary person asks for a favor → the streamer delivers.
  - Humbled → fights back.
- Heavy **color grade** (contrast, sharpening, slight glow), **music-driven**, often with slowed/phonk audio. MrBeastClips used "Sem Tempo (Ultra Slowed)".
- A **glowing title card** early, e.g. "ISHOWSPEED IN WWE (2026)" or "2025" → "2026" year labels in "then vs now" edits.
- Captions: 2–3 word uppercase lines with glow, with the key word in **yellow** ("**CHAT** NO WAY").
- Themes that hit 150M+: **wholesome / respect / loyalty to fans**, **aura / comeback**, "we made the right person famous".

### C. Top-N countdown
*Clips.Delivery: "Top 5 Funniest Fanmail" 38M, "Top 5 Fanmail Toys" 26M*
- Overlay at the top in TikTok-native font: **"Fan mail pt5 / Top 5 funny moments"**, with an empty numbered list 1–5.
- The list **fills in as each clip plays** (5 → 1). It's an open loop, so people stay to see #1.
- Series numbering (pt1, pt2, … pt5) builds a franchise and repeat viewers.
- 40–72s.

### D. Context-box viral clip
*KickClipsBR (34M, 25M, 19M)*
- **White rounded text box at the top**, black text + 2 emojis, describing the situation. Example: "Trocando carne pronta por uma crua 🤢🤮" ("Swapping cooked meat for raw").
- **Trending licensed audio** under it. The 34M video used "Don't Worry Be Happy".
- **17–24s**, and ultra-short, vague ALL-CAPS titles: "NO WAY 🤦🏼‍♂️🤔", "SCAM PROHIBITED 🚫❌".
- Works across languages because the story is visual.

### E. Storytelling / curiosity podcast clip (best for Hindi / Indian podcasts)
*RealTalk Clips (12M), TRS Clips (11.8M)*
- **45–112s**. Starts **mid-story**, e.g. "This was done in Russia around 1947…", with no intro.
- Topics: dark history, horror, crime, secrets, "reality of…", health myths ("juice in diabetes" 11.8M), celebrity secrets ("Akshay Kumar's fitness SECRET" 6.5M).
- Captions: 2-word chunks, black text in a **white rounded box**, lower-center.
- **Link the full episode** with YouTube's "related video" link on the Short. RealTalk links its long video on every Short.

### F. Language arbitrage (under-used opportunity)
*SpeedMoments-s2r: Russian re-edits of Speed clips, only 49 uploads, 129K subs, top 5.3M*
- Take a globally famous creator's moments and re-caption and re-title them in **another language market** (Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Spanish, Indonesian).
- Each Short uses one language only, either English or Russian, in both the title and the captions.
- This is a lower-competition way into a huge personality.

## 4. HOOK RULES (0–3 seconds)

Measured from the transcripts: **in almost every top video the first spoken word lands between 0.0s and 1.0s.** No top video had a logo intro, "wait for it", or silent setup (the one exception is below).

| Rule | Parameter |
|---|---|
| **H1. Start mid-action** | First word or visual event at **≤0.5s**. Cut everything before the first interesting frame. |
| **H2. Premise in the first sentence** | The first line has to explain the situation on its own. Openers that worked: someone about to do something ("I'm about to join K's stream"), a challenge question ("Can you name three countries?"), a bet ("If I gave you a one-pound weight, how many curls…"), a provocation (an insult or claim that demands a reply). |
| **H3. Face + emotion on frame 1** | The first frame is a close-up of a face mid-expression (shock, laugh, anger), or the most striking visual (a man in a PRIME bottle costume in a WWE ring). |
| **H4. Hook text only if the audio doesn't hook** | If the first line isn't self-explanatory, add an on-screen context line (format D box, or a format B title card) for the first **1–3s**. |
| **H5. Music-only openings are allowed only in format B** | MrBeastClips opened with ~9s of music montage, but the on-screen visual (a "2025" label and a 100M plaque) was the hook. Never open on a static or boring frame. |
| **H6. Open a loop** | The first 3s should raise a question the ending answers: will he find the earring? What is #1? What does she answer? |

## 5. STRUCTURE & LENGTH RULES

**Measured on 90 top Shorts from 15 channels:**
- **Median length 37s**, mean 38.5s, middle half **24–54s**.
- 0–20s: 16 videos · 20–30s: 17 · 30–45s: 26 · 45–61s: 27 · **over 61s: only 4**
- So **86 of 90 top Shorts were ≤ 60s**, even though Shorts can now run 3 minutes.

| Rule | Parameter |
|---|---|
| **S1. Target length by format** | A: **15–45s** · B: **50–60s** · C: **40–70s** · D: **15–25s** · E: **45–90s** (up to 120s only if the story is gripping) |
| **S2. Never pad** | Cut every pause, "um", and dead air. Exception: a deliberate **silence before the punchline** (Fresh&Fit's "Name 3 Countries" holds an awkward pause with music, then the punchline). Silence has to be *the joke*, not waste. |
| **S3. End on the peak** | Hard-cut on the punchline, reaction, or payoff line. **No outro, no "subscribe", no end card.** Every top clip ends abruptly, which triggers replays (replays count as views since March 2025). |
| **S4. Seamless loop (bonus)** | Where possible, make the last frame flow into the first so replays feel natural. |
| **S5. One idea per Short** | One moment, one joke, one story. Two unrelated moments means two Shorts. |
| **S6. Escalate** | In multi-beat clips each beat should be bigger than the last. SpeedKaiClip's 109M clip escalates insult by insult until Kai reacts. |

## 6. VISUAL / EDITING RULES

### Framing
| Rule | Parameter |
|---|---|
| **V1. Full-bleed 9:16** | Fill the screen. **No black bars, no blurred-background letterbox.** None of the top videos used one. |
| **V2. Crop to the speaker's face** | Face in the upper-middle third. Re-crop at each speaker change (active-speaker crop). |
| **V3. Stacked split-screen for two-person moments** | Top half: streamer/host A. Bottom half: B, or the gameplay/stream being reacted to. SpeedKaiClip does this for every Speed×Kai interaction. |
| **V4. Punch-in zooms** | Zoom 10–20% on reactions and punchlines. |
| **V5. Keep screen-safe zones clear** | Nothing important in the bottom ~20% (title/channel UI) or the right ~12% (like/comment buttons). |

### Captions (the most consistent pattern across all winners)
| Rule | Parameter |
|---|---|
| **C1. Always burn in captions** | Every top Short had burned-in captions. Most viewers scroll with sound off. |
| **C2. 1–3 words at a time** | Karaoke / word-by-word. Never full sentences. |
| **C3. Font** | Bold condensed sans in **UPPERCASE**: Anton, Bebas Neue, Impact, Montserrat Black, or The Bold Font. |
| **C4. Color** | White with a thick black stroke or shadow is the default. **Yellow** (#FFE600-ish) for emphasis or for the whole caption (Full Send, SpeedKaiClip). Formats E/whatever: black text in a white rounded box, or white text in a black box. |
| **C5. Different color per speaker** | SpeedKaiClip: Speed in yellow, Kai in a teal/blue box. It makes the dialogue readable on mute. |
| **C6. Highlight key words** | Money, names, and insults get the accent color or a size bump. |
| **C7. Position** | Center of the screen (~45–60% height), over the chest/face area. Stay out of the bottom UI zone. |
| **C8. Accuracy** | Fix auto-caption mistakes. Censor slurs and profanity with asterisks or bleeps, which is also needed for advertiser-friendliness. |

### Style layer (format B, recommended for A as well)
- Color grade: more contrast, more saturation, light sharpening, subtle glow or bloom.
- Short title card with glow in the first 1–3s: SUBJECT + CONTEXT + YEAR.
- Emoji/sticker pops on emotional beats (❤️‍🩹 on the hug, 💀 on the fail).
- Sound design: whoosh on cuts, bass hit on the payoff, a vine-boom or record-scratch on cringe.

### Branding
- **Watermark:** semi-transparent **@handle** in bold outlined font, placed at mid-lower center (deagzzz does this). It deters re-uploaders and gives free branding when clips get reposted.
- **Consistent look:** the same font, colors, and grade on every video. A viewer should recognize your channel in 1 second.

## 7. AUDIO RULES
| Rule | Parameter |
|---|---|
| **A1. Dialogue first** | For formats A, C, E: normalize dialogue loud and clear, with no music fighting it. |
| **A2. Music bed** | Low background music (~-25 dB under the voice) is optional in A and required in B and D. |
| **A3. Trending audio** | Formats B and D: use **trending/slowed/phonk tracks from YouTube's own Shorts audio library.** Library tracks show the song tag under the video, as MrBeastClips and KickClipsBR had, and don't trigger Content ID claims that take your revenue. |
| **A4. No copyrighted music off-platform** | Never rip songs from Spotify. Use the in-app Shorts audio picker or royalty-free sources. |
| **A5. SFX** | Whoosh on transitions, boom on reveals, laugh or ding on punchlines. Keep them subtle and don't spam. |

## 8. TITLE RULES

| Rule | Parameter |
|---|---|
| **T1. Length** | **3–8 words.** Top titles: "Kai Cenat finally grew Taller!🥹", "Name 3 Countries", "Theo Von Is a Menace". |
| **T2. Name first** | Put the creator's name first. It's search and suggestion fuel: "Speed…", "Kai Cenat…", "Theo Von…", "MrBeast…". Exception: format B/D curiosity titles. |
| **T3. Verb + outcome** | [Person] [strong verb] [surprising object]. "Speed **tried to get** KaiCenat **permanently banned**", "Ray **finally uses** fire extinguisher in Kai room". |
| **T4. 1–2 emojis at the end** | 😭 🤣 💀 🔥 🥹 ❤️‍🩹 😱 🤯. Match the emoji to the emotion (😭 funny-cringe, 🥹❤️‍🩹 wholesome, 💀🔥 aura/crazy). |
| **T5. CAPS on 1–2 words only** | "Katt Williams **Can't Handle** Theo Von", "Speed **LOSES IT**". All-caps titles only for format D. |
| **T6. Curiosity > description** | Don't give the ending away. "The ENDING is crazy 💀🔥" (200M), "We made the right person famous 💖" (231M), "He even got it cleaned, mad respect ❤️‍🩹" (285M). |
| **T7. Celebrity crossover in the title** | If a second famous person appears, name them: "Theo Von Meets Mark Zuckerberg" (26M), "Ronaldo Should Never Challenge MrBeast" (16M), "Kai Cenat finds Ice Spice in GTA 5" (12M). |
| **T8. Words that win** | finally, tried to, can't handle, caught, crashed out, LOSES IT, no chill, menace, aura, -100,000 aura, cooked, never forgets, broke (someone), savage, INSANE, $[number]. |
| **T9. Avoid** | Long, rambling titles (kickclipofficial's 20-word titles get ~1K views), formula suffixes ("… | Figuring Out #Shorts"), spelling mistakes, and titles made only of hashtags. |

## 9. DESCRIPTION, HASHTAGS & METADATA

- **Description:** repeat the title + one context sentence, then **credit** the creator with links to their main channel, Twitch/Kick, and Instagram. SpeedKaiClip and TheoRoganShorts do this. It's goodwill with the creator and supports the "transformation + attribution" case.
- **Hashtags:** **2–5**, in the form `#creatorname #topic #edit` (e.g. `#ishowspeed #wholesome #edit`, `#TheoVon #MikeTyson #Shorts`). More than 6 hashtags looks spammy.
- **Tags (keywords field):** creator name variants ("kai cenat", "kaicenat", "kai cenat clips", "kai cenat funny moments"), guest names, and "[creator] shorts".
- **Related video link (Shorts):** point it to our own long-form or a relevant Short (RealTalk does this every time).
- **Pinned comment:** a question that invites debate ("Who was wrong here? 👇"). Comments drive distribution.
- **Language:** set the video language correctly. For format F, write the title and description entirely in the target language.

## 10. CONTENT SELECTION RULES (what to clip)

**This is where views are won or lost.** Score each candidate moment before editing it.

| **Conflict / roast / getting humbled** | Whatever "OnlyFans girls get roasted" 21M, Fresh&Fit "Myron Debates…" 9M |
| **Cringe / ignorance** | Fresh&Fit "Name 3 Countries" 38M, Jynxzi singing on TV 32M |

| **Big numbers / money / stakes** | Full Send "MrBeast's $25,000 challenge" 49M, "$20K Shoe or Pink Hair" 34M, Jynxzi "lost $500" 21M |
| **Absurd / chaotic** | "Ray finally uses fire extinguisher in Kai room" 50M, "KaiCenat does the egg challenge" 73M |
| **Milestone / comeback / aura** | "MrBeast Completed YouTube" (500M subs) 66M, "iShowSpeed Finally Fought Back" 313M |
| **Dark curiosity (podcasts)** | RealTalk "Zombie experiment" 12M; long-form "GB Road ka sach" 16M |
| **Practical myth-busting (Hindi)** | TRS "Diabetes mein juice" 11.8M |

### Scoring gate (use it on every clip, and publish only at ≥ 7/10)
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Understandable in 3s with sound OFF | 2 |
| Strong emotion peak (laugh, shock, awww, anger) | 2 |
| Famous second person / crossover | 1 |
| Clear stakes (money, challenge, conflict, loss) | 1 |
| Clean ending on a punchline or payoff | 1 |
| Trending right now (≤48h old moment) | 1 |
| Broad appeal: a non-fan would still watch | 1 |
| Fits our channel's personality / niche | 1 |

### Content anti-patterns (seen in the flops)
- Inside-baseball talk that needs context (kickclipofficial's UFC small talk: ~1K views).
- Generic advice with no emotion (Raj Shamani Clips' business Shorts top out at 131K).
- Filler topics that don't fit the niche (RealTalk's travel Shorts: ~1K views).
- Clips that are "funny if you were there". If it needs backstory, it fails H2.

### Speed rule
- For **live streamers**, post the moment **within hours** of it happening. The first uploads of a moment collect the trending wave, and later copies fight over what's left.
- For **podcasts**, post clips on or right after the episode's release day, then work through the back catalog for evergreen moments.

## 11. MONETIZATION, POLICY & RIGHTS (don't skip this)

- **Reused content policy:** clip, compilation, and reaction channels **stay monetizable only with "significant original commentary, modifications, or educational/entertainment value."** Straight re-uploads get rejected or demonetized.
- **15 Jul 2025:** "repetitious content" was renamed **"inauthentic content"** and is now judged at **channel level**, targeting mass-produced, templated, minimal-effort channels.
- **Jan 2026:** the first large enforcement wave removed 16 big channels (~4.7B lifetime views). Compilations "with no meaningful transformation" were a named risk.
- **YPP rewrite announced 10 Aug 2026, effective 1 Feb 2027:** adds activity requirements (e.g. 1M Shorts views per 90 days, or minimum upload counts) to stay in the Partner Program.
- **Shorts views:** since 31 Mar 2025 every play/replay counts as a view, but **revenue and YPP eligibility use "engaged views"**. Retention still pays.

**Our transformation checklist.** Every video must have at least 3 of these:
1. Re-framed 9:16 with active-speaker crops or split-screen.
2. Custom styled captions (our font and colors).
3. Added context: a title card, text box, or countdown list.
4. Multi-source story assembly (format B) or ranking (format C).
5. Color grade + sound design + music.
6. Translation or localization (format F).
7. Our own narrative framing: an intro line, a "then vs now" comparison, or a payoff card.

**Rights:**
- Prefer creators who **allow or encourage clipping** (many streamers run official clipping programs; Clips.Delivery says Jynxzi follows its TikTok). Ask or check before building a channel on one person.
- Always credit the source in the description.
- A "fair use disclaimer" (SpeedMoments has one) is **not** legal protection. Transformation is what counts.
- Expect occasional takedowns and don't dispute blindly.
- Don't use the creator's logo or name as if we were the official channel. Our channel name should read clearly as a fan/clip channel (e.g. "SpeedKaiClip", "Theo Rogan Shorts").

## 12. LONG-FORM RULES (secondary)

| Channel type | Does long-form work? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Streamer clip channels | **Mostly no** | Streamer Clip Vault documentaries: 1K–100K. RossAdinClips' recent long-form: 1K–10K. |
| Gaming highlight compilations | Only when they're old/established | Livestream Moments "Funniest Fail Moments #1": 3.3M, but recent uploads get 30K. |
| **Podcast clips on dark/taboo/curiosity topics** | **Yes** | RealTalk "GB Road ka ghinona sach" **15.9M**; TheoVonClips "How 'free' p*rn sites really work" **8.8M**, "Jordan Peterson on alcohol" 8.8M. |
| Debate/conflict podcasts | Yes, 10–30 min | Whatever "Charlie Kirk debates feminists" 3.7M. |

**If we do long-form:**
- **8–20 min** single-topic segments.
- Title = the topic plus a secret, danger, or truth angle: "The Dark Side of…", "…Ka Sach", "How X Really Works".
- The thumbnail should show a face with emotion + 2–4 words.
- Use Shorts as trailers for the long video (related-video link).

## 13. POSTING & OPERATIONS

| Rule | Parameter |
|---|---|
| **O1. Volume** | Target **2–4 Shorts/day** per channel. Winners have 500–1,700+ uploads. |
| **O2. Consistency > bursts** | Post daily. Don't disappear for weeks (GhostRyderPlug drifted and died). |
| **O3. Series** | Build repeatable franchises: "Top 5 Fanmail pt1…ptN", "Speed vs [Country]", "Theo Von vs [Guest]". |
| **O4. Measure** | After 48h, compare each Short against the channel median. Double down on topics and formats that beat the median by 3× or more, and stop the ones that stay under 0.5×. |
| **O5. Recycle winners** | A 10M+ moment can come back as a new cut (different angle, a "then vs now" version, a Top-5 inclusion) weeks later. |
| **O6. Cross-post** | The same edit goes to TikTok and Instagram Reels (remove the YouTube-only elements). |
| **O7. One channel = one creator/duo** | Want another creator? Start another channel. |
